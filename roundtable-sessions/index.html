    GENUINE CONNECT
    We are thrilled to invite you the morning of August 1st, for a day of inspiration, collaboration, and innovation at the Genuine Connect Event, part of the Genuine World Cup Houston 2024. This unique gathering is designed to foster meaningful dialogue and share valuable insights on the inclusion and empowerment of athletes with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) and autism.

    Through engaging panel discussions, networking opportunities, and interactive sessions, we aim to create a platform where ideas can flourish and impactful connections can be made.

    Together, we can drive positive change and build a more inclusive future. Thank you for being a part of this important initiative!
    PANEL 1: ELIMINATING BARRIERS FOR EQUAL ACCESS TO BENEFITS FOR IDD AND AUTISM
    Understand the gaps for equality and discuss strategies to remove obstacles and ensure equitable access to benefits for individuals with IDD and autism.

    Speakers:
    • Ron Lucey, Executive Director on Texas Governor’s Committee on People with Disabilities
    • Dr. Evanthe Collins, Vice President IDD services division at the Harris Center for Mental Health and IDD
    • Christopher Banks, President and CEO Autism Society of America
    PANEL 2: HOW TO EMPLOY AND TRAIN?
    Learn from successful companies and organizations their innovative methods for training and employing individuals with IDD and autism, promoting their career development and independence.

    Speakers:
    • Jose Velasco, SVP Autism Society of America, SAP Autism program Founder (retired)
    • Jeff Miller, CEO of Potentia
    • Charles Njuguna, President and CEO of the Center for Pursuit
    • Thomas D'Eri, COO Rising Tide
    PANEL 3: USING SPORTS AND FITNESS AS A VENUE FOR INCLUSION
    Learn how sports and fitness programs can be utilized to foster social inclusion and empower individuals with IDD and autism.

    Speakers:
    • Olga de La Fuente, Director of FUNDACIÓN LALIGA
    • Alberto Puig, Director Fundación Mi Alex. Be My Referee project
    • Tim Martin, CEO of Special Olympics Texas
    • John Watson, Founder and President Bloom Fitness
